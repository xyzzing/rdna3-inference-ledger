# Campaign update — Nov 12/13, 2026: where the 94K decode penalty actually lives, and the close on the current stack

One overnight window + two follow-ups, **zero engine changes** for the attribution (the
production binary already carried every diagnostic gate — checked by strings before writing
any instrumentation), and one 2-minute-10-second GPU screen that closed the last bitwise
candidate. This campaign answered a question the ledger had left as a `TODO` since October:
**what exactly is the deep-context decode penalty, per committed token, matched?**

## The matched depth table (first ever)

Deep vs short decode, same instance, paired batteries (an A/B bracket spread up to ~15% per
cell → only within-battery paired deltas are valid):

| fixture case | deep-vs-short delta | reading |
|---|---|---|
| pooled | **+1.176 ms/token (+11.1%)** | the real, matched depth penalty |
| p-code / p-zh | commits/round cases | offered **width shrinks at depth** (N/W −14%), ms/round flat-to-down |
| p-reg | small per-round case (+0.78 ms/round) | the only transfer-shaped signal |

Other firsts from the same batteries: cold deep prefill **3,435 tok/s @97.9K**; KV spill
**168–354 MiB to RAM per request** at 98.5–99.3% VRAM hit; and a **definitional correction**
the ledger records openly: engine decode at 70–98K measures **79–94 tok/s** on the matched
protocol — not the 43–47 quoted earlier. Both figures are now in the record with a provenance
gap note (different engine vintage and token-counting definition; do not mix them).

## The promotion budget halved

With a real matched baseline, the historical **≥1.020×** gate translates to **0.22–0.24
ms/token** at 94K — half the 0.42–0.46 the older arithmetic assumed. Every candidate on the
board was re-priced against the tighter bar.

## Attribution: zero engine code, one surprise, one close

- **True window count**: 184–185 windows per 400-token deep request (avg width T = 2.47).
  *[Corrected Nov 14: this is the p-code/94k cell's **first-two-requests** figure, not the
  deep-request population — all 18 traced requests span W 112–194, avg T 2.32–3.94. Per-cell
  values govern; caught by the fingerprint re-audit, see the
  [corrections log](methodology.md#corrections-log).]*
- **GPU-reach wait ≈ 79% of the ~30 ms window at BOTH depths — flat.** Not the depth
  mechanism. And per the owner's review: this figure is **host-blocking, not device idleness** —
  it must not be read as "the GPU is idle 79% of the time" or used to fund a runtime rewrite.
- **The 94K penalty is PROPOSAL-SIDE**: the offered width shrinks at depth while acceptance
  holds at matched width (the first-lost-token audit, verdict A — run with a zero-new-code
  capture: the prod binary's own width/probability diagnostics). Verification-side: closed.
- **The draft head kernel, priced**: ≈0.46–0.50 of the draft graph = 4.9–5.9% of window =
  0.6–0.8 ms/token. A 2× head kernel would save ≈2.5–3.0% of window wall — the only
  arithmetically qualified candidate.

## The screen that closed it

The only bitwise-preserving head-kernel candidate (a four-plane SoA repack of the Q6_K head,
with an in-tree bitwise self-test) rooflines at **≤1.14×** — 2.5–3.8× short of the bar before
measuring. It was screened anyway (2 min 10 s of GPU): **bitwise-equal outputs, −6.8% to
−40.6% at every width** (11 paired samples each). The SoA planes defeat the access pattern
the GGUF-layout kernel is tuned for; +497 MiB of repacked head bought nothing. **Closed
without a battery.**

Also closed this campaign: the expert/KV off-card split (both pools' off-card traffic
near-empty), the two-row KV share (the replay input does not exist in saved records), and the
candidate-C exposure question (closed on measured exposure). Avalon-class serving stayed
NOT_RUN on a memory gate; its source audit is pinned with one item needing a governance ruling.

## The close, and what reopens it

**The campaign is closed on the current stack**: no qualifying candidate for the
0.22–0.24 ms/token bar exists on this fingerprint. Reopening requires a *new mechanism class*:

1. **proposal-width-at-depth** — why the offered width shrinks at depth (the smallest missing
   counter needs zero new code: a full-vocabulary top-2 capture in a short, untimed
   correctness window; then a config-only `--spec-min-p` sweep at depth);
2. a **serialization-floor attack** — with the recorded caveat that the "floor is attackable"
   premise is itself unproven until the avoidable non-overlapped slice is measured.

Owner-reviewed corrections are part of the record: the "79% waiting" framing overstated
(host-blocking ≠ device idleness); an FP4 requant premise was **backwards for gfx1100**
(IQ3_S at ~110 B/block is already denser than 4-bit's 128 B + scales, and the card has no
FP4 matrix path); fp8 KV is not "half of int8". The adopted next-experiment order:
first-lost-token audit (done) → one bounded proposal experiment if justified → an IQ3_S
integer-dot replay if priced — with format changes, runtime rewrites, and a second GPU as
behind-or-separate tracks.
